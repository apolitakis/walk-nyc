Walk NYC privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Walk NYC is a small, non-commercial audio walking tour app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

The app uses your location on your phone to start each stop's narration when you arrive. In the iPhone app, the stops of the tour you are walking are registered with iOS so they can trigger while your phone is locked. Your location is not sent anywhere, except when you write a feedback note (below).

Feedback notes

If you write a note with the pencil button, the app sends it to the Walk NYC server with: the text you wrote, the tour, stop and sentence playing at the time, your position and its accuracy (if the app has one), the app version, and your device's browser identifier (user agent). Notes are not linked to your name, Apple ID or any account. They are read only by the app's author, to fix and improve the tours. Notes that can't be sent right away are kept on your phone until they can.

Other services

The app and its recorded narration are served by Cloudflare. The map uses tiles from OpenStreetMap. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your phone downloads from them.

On your phone

Settings, unsent notes and the titles of the stops you are walking are stored on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Questions or deletion

Send a feedback note from the app asking for a note to be removed, or contact the person who invited you to test.
