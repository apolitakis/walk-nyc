# Walk NYC

Audio walking tours of Manhattan and Brooklyn: 22 neighborhoods, 162 GPS-triggered stops, about 7.5 hours of narration on storied companies, history, people and architecture, including both halves of Central Park.

- **Use it:** https://walk-nyc.pages.dev (Safari on iPhone; Add to Home Screen)
- **How it's put together, and how to change it:** see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md), [SHIPPING.md](SHIPPING.md), [voices/README.md](voices/README.md), [ios/BUILD-iOS.md](ios/BUILD-iOS.md)
